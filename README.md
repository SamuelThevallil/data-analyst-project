# data-analyst-project

Amazon India — Executive Sales & Profitability Dashboard. Project Overview: 

This project was developed as part of the Sapphire IQ Data Analyst Internship. The primary goal is to design an executive-level, non-technical interactive dashboard in Microsoft Excel for business stakeholders (Manoj & Manager Ravi). The dashboard synthesizes 10,000 transactional records (Jan 2024 – Aug 2026) to track revenue drivers, profitability, order fulfillment health, customer payment behavior, and geographic performance.   

Business Problem & Requirements: Management required a unified reporting view to monitor performance without navigating raw dataset complexity.

Key business objectives addressed include:

Overall Performance: Evaluating revenue, net profit, average basket size, and overall profit margin trends over time.

Category & Product Drivers: Identifying high-margin categories and top-performing SKUs to optimize inventory allocation.

Order Status & Revenue Loss: Tracking order fulfillment success rates and quantifying revenue leakage from cancellations and returns.

Channel & Geographic Insights: Analyzing performance across fulfillment modes, payment options, and regional states.

**Detailed Analysis & Findings:**

1. **Overall Sales & Profitability:**

**Revenue Trend:** Revenue generation remained robust across the 32 months, driven largely by high-ticket electronics. 

**Profitability:** Maintained a healthy profit margin of 21.29% overall. 

2. **Category & Product Performance:**

**Electronics & Mobiles** is the dominant revenue engine, generating ₹123.57M (79.3% of total revenue) and ₹26.31M in profit.

**Home & Kitchen** ranks second with ₹17.97M in revenue. 

**Top 5 Products by Sales:**

(A)**Laptop Backpack **— ₹ 26.53 M

(B)**Power Bank 20000mAh** — ₹ 25.55 M

(C) **5 G Smartphone** — ₹ 24.33 M

(D) **Wireless Earbuds** — ₹ 23.85 M

(E) **Smartwatch **— ₹23.31M3

**Order Status & Revenue Loss Fulfillment Efficiency:** 81.47% (8,147) of orders were successfully delivered, and 8.68% (868) are currently in transit (shipped).

**Revenue Leakage:** Cancellation Rate: 5.00% (500 orders).

**Return Rate:** 4.85% (485 orders).

Combined order loss accounts for ~9.85% of potential fulfillment volume, emphasizing a need to improve pre-dispatch confirmation and product description clarity.

3. **Payment, Fulfillment & Regional Performance. Payment Channels:**

UPI leads as the preferred payment method, accounting for 50.4% of total sales (₹78.52M across 4,972 orders), followed by Credit/Debit Cards (₹31.07M) and Cash on Delivery (₹23.10M). 

**Fulfillment Channels:** Amazon (FBA) handles the vast majority of volume (69.0% of total sales, ₹107.53M), delivering superior scale compared to Seller Flex (₹31.20M) and Merchant FBM (₹17.06M). 

**Top Geographic Markets:**

**Maharashtra:** ₹29.55M Sales | 1,840 Orders

**Karnataka:** ₹24.42M Sales | 1,515 Orders

**Delhi:** ₹22.36M Sales | 1,444 Orders

**Tamil Nadu:** ₹17.89M Sales | 1,109 Orders

**Uttar Pradesh:** ₹14.14M Sales | 991 Orders

**Actionable Business Recommendations:**

**I)** **Optimize FBA Allocation:** FBA generates 69% of revenue and has lower dispute rates; shifting eligible Merchant (FBM) inventory to FBA will improve fulfillment speed and lower cancellation rates.

**II)** **Mitigate COD Cancellations & Returns:** Implement pre-dispatch verification (SMS/WhatsApp) for Cash on Delivery orders to reduce the ~9.85% revenue loss across returns and cancellations. 

**III)** **Regional Marketing Focus:** Concentrating promotional ad spend in high-volume states (Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Delhi) will maximize ROI.
